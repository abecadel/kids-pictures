# Kids' pictures

100 everyday topics a small child may ask to see (animals, vehicles, nature, space, food, places), with up to 8 pictures each, 712 in all. Each picture was picked from Wikimedia Commons (mostly its reviewed *Quality images*) and checked by eye for a small child: the thing is clear and easy to find, nothing scary, sad or unsuitable.

Made for a hobby picture viewer on a small round screen.
